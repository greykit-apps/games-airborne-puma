# Airborne Puma

**Twelve missions behind enemy lines.** A remake of *Airborne Ranger* (MicroProse, 1987, Commodore 64), redrawn as modern pixel art in the original's tilted overhead view. The ranger is a puma and the enemy are wolves.

**Play:** [greykit.com/games/airbornepuma](https://greykit.com/games/airbornepuma/)

![The title: the depot from the air, the Osprey crossing with a ranger just out under his parafoil, and the menu](screenshots/title.png)

## How it plays

The Osprey flies you over the mission area. Drop three supply pods where you'll want them, then jump on the green light. On the ground the clock is running: reach the objective, do the job, and get back to the pickup in time. Twelve missions across desert, temperate and arctic zones, with every map laid out new each time. Veteran rangers keep their rank, decorations and service record, and a campaign plays all twelve in order.

**Classic** plays as the 1987 game did. **Field**, the default, adds a minimap, target arrows, enemy awareness markers and other aids, each of which can be switched on or off.

![On the ground at the depot: the puma ranger at the wire below the fuel dump, wolf soldiers with their fields of view, and the HUD](screenshots/ground.png)

## Keys

A desktop game for keyboard or gamepad, in a window at least 760 × 480. `?` on the title screen lists every key; `M` is sound.

## Saves

Kept in the browser's `localStorage` under keys starting `airbornepuma_`. **Settings → Forget everything** clears them.
